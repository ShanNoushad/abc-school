About ABC

One campus, every stage of learning

From a nursery classroom to a college lecture hall, ABC Schools & College is built so a child can grow up on one campus — taught by people who watch them grow, not just pass through.

We keep class sizes small, mentorship real, and results honest. Our students leave knowing not just what to think, but how to.

Small classrooms

An average of 28 students per section, from Grade 1 onward.

Mentor faculty

Every student is assigned a personal academic mentor.

One campus

Nursery through undergraduate, on a single 12-acre site.

Real outcomes

96% board pass rate, 100+ college placements yearly.

Programs

A path for every stage

Four stages, one philosophy — build fundamentals early, widen choice later, and never let a student feel lost in the transition.

Grades 1–5

Primary School

Play-led fundamentals in reading, number sense, and curiosity — taught by teachers trained in early-years pedagogy.

Grades 6–10

Secondary School

Board-aligned academics with hands-on labs and project work.

Grades 11–12

Senior Secondary

Science, Commerce & Humanities streams with career counselling built in.

Undergraduate

ABC College

B.Com, B.Sc, B.A. and BBA programs with industry-linked internships and a 100+ recruiter placement cell.
